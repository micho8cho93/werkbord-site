Four house rules.
Kept by the software, not by willpower.

  1. 01

    It asks instead of guessing.

    When an agent reaches a real decision, it stops and puts the question on its card, with the answers it can see. One click and it carries on. Work that can’t continue is marked blocked, not buried in a log you’ll read on Friday.

    Needs inputmy-app · 4M

    Two callers still read the old 24-hour session TTL. Keep it for them?

    Keep 24 hoursMove both to 12 hours
  2. 02

    Nothing merges without you.

    Every task runs in its own Git worktree, so your checkout never shifts under your feet. Finished work waits in Review with its branch, its commits and every changed line. Merging is a button only you press, and it checks the repository again the moment you do.

    Fix date picker off-by-one

    Ready for review

    werkbord/fix-date-picker-off-by-one · 1 commit ahead

    Merge…Review
  3. 03

    Your code stays home.

    There is no Werkbord account and no server of ours in the middle. The board, the database and your agents’ sign-ins live on your Mac. Your phone reaches it over your own private network, not through someone else’s cloud.

    Your Maccontroller · agents · databaseyour tailnetYour phonethe same board

    Nothing else in the picture. That is the point.

  4. 04

    The work doesn’t clock out when you do.

    Werkbord runs as a background service. Close the window, quit the app, and your agents and schedules keep going for as long as the Mac is awake. Put tomorrow’s chores on the calendar and they start on time, in order, without you.

Connect your phone

Three steps from download
to an agent at work.

  1. 1

    Open the app

    Drag Werkbord to Applications and open it. It sets itself up in a few seconds and starts the background service that keeps working when the window is closed.

  2. 2

    Add a repository

    Choose a folder on your Mac, or connect GitHub and pick one from your list. Each repository becomes a project with its own board, calendar and Git view.

  3. 3

    Write the first task

    Say what done looks like, choose Claude Code or Codex, and press start. It uses the agent you already have, signed in with your own account. Werkbord never asks for an API key.
