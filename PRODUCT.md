# Werkbord

Werkbord is a local-first team workspace for AI agents. People plan and approve the work on one board, agents do it in their own Git worktrees, and nothing merges without a person. It organizes tasks, agent runs, schedules, and reviews around local Git repositories. Today it connects to Claude Code and Codex; support for other models is planned. Phone access lets users follow the same work remotely while execution remains on their computer.

Werkbord Team coordinates people, ticket ownership, reviews, and reported repository state. Each teammate keeps their own local runner and credentials. Product managers and designers write tickets, follow progress and sign off from a browser; simpler flows for people who do not code are planned. The public site offers early access through a waitlist. Documentation also serves people who already have Team access and need installation instructions.

The website should quickly explain the essential individual and team workflows. Interactive product examples carry the detail. Keep marketing copy concise, concrete, and free of em dashes. Technical figures must come from product evidence and state any qualification, such as performance measured in tests.

Positioning: the site speaks of a team workspace for AI agents that bridges people who code and people who do not. State only what ships today. Planned work (other models, simpler flows for non-developers) may appear once, labelled as planned, in the FAQ and llms.txt, never in a headline, title or description. `node --test scripts/test-seo.cjs` enforces that.

Preserve the established pixel mark, Geist typography, neutral surfaces, cobalt and amber accents, and light/dark themes. Use restrained motion and respect reduced-motion preferences. Public footer navigation contains only Team, Install, and Docs.

The documentation is task-oriented and based on the product repository's installation, execution, scheduling, phone, runner, and Team guides. It needs native pages, searchable topics, usable mobile navigation, and copyable commands.
